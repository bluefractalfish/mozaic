about

Mozaic therapies was founded by Kim Gershon a licensed clinician with training in both social work and psychology. Kim has used integrated approaches to working with adolescents, adults, families, groups and couples. Kim is a licensed social worker in both MA and CO and is currently engaged in plant medicine certification through the Integrative Psychiatric Institute. Kim currently holds a facilitator training license in Plant Medicine through CO.

programs

ketamine for practitioners

Join a group of therapists, nurses and other practitioners to explore the benefits of Ketamine assisted psychotherapy (KAP). Through group process, you will learn about the KAP experience from within. Meet with other practitioners as we set intentions, goals and explore the benefits of Ketamine and other psychedelic therapies. Individual sessions are available for an additional fee.

details
  • meet with Kim for introductory session and to prepare Journey Clinical (JC) referral
  • schedule and attend JC psychiatry appointment to receive Ketamine prescription (this will be mailed to you directly)
  • prepare intentions for your KAP experience
  • on the day of the KAP experience, you will need a ride to join the group KAP session
  • together with up to 4 practitioners, we will discuss the highs and lows of being a practitioner in today’s world
  • briefly process individual goals and intentions for the journey; share concerns about the upcoming experience
  • each person will be invited to bring an eye mask, ear buds and a playlist (this can also be provided). you will be given a private space for the journey portion of the group experience. journey portion will last approximately 75-90 minutes
  • after the Ketamine journey we will come together to process and integrate the experience and set goals/intentions for the following days of increased neuroplasticity
  • the group experience will last approximately 3 ½ hours including the KAP journey

individual or group psilocybin retreat

Psilocybin retreat is an opportunity to look at your goals and aspirations and dedicate a focused effort into manifesting change.

details
  • you have the opportunity to determine your lodging and Kim will come to your space for the journey day. Prep and integration sessions may be done in person or remotely
  • if you desire a couples or group experience we can create the opportunity for you and your partner/family/friends
  • prior to dosing day, meet with Kim to discuss life updates, intentions and goals as well as dosing plan. In the case of a group experience this will be done individually (in advance 60 minutes/per person) and with the group on the eve of the dosing day (90 minutes)
  • on the day of the journey, we will come together to discuss goals and intentions. We will engage in breath exercises, and possibly stretching, movement, nature and/or art. During the early phase of the journey, you will retreat to a meditative space with an eye mask and pre-determined playlist. The meditative portion of the journey will last about 60-120 minutes. I will be present as you journey and as you return to the therapeutic space. We might engage with integration and meditative space alternatively. Toward the end of the journey, we will discuss goals, intentions and mindfulness (5-6 hours)
  • on the next day, we will meet again to discuss and integrate the journey, process insights and develop next steps (90 minutes)
  • follow up as desired/needed (not included in retreat fee)

to enhance the retreat experience you may want to include the following on the days after your journey:

  • massage
  • reiki
  • yoga
  • hiking
  • lodging with hot tub
  • horseback riding
